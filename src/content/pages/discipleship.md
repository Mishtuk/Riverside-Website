*And Jesus came and said to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." - Matthew 28:18-20 ESV*

We believe that the best structure for making disciples in the church is through small 3-5 person groups who meet consistently to study the Bible and grow together with a goal of multiplying groups and inviting more people into relationship with God and one another.

## Current Study: Hebrews

Our current Discipleship Group study is walking through the book of Hebrews alongside of a deep dive into the stories of Faith from Hebrews Chapter 11. From week to week we will be utilizing both of these studies. We have also provided versions of the study guide that include the scriptures for the week. Use whichever version is most helpful for you!
