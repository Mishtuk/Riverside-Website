Baptism is the immersion of a believer in water in the name of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. It is an act of obedience symbolizing the believer's faith in a crucified, buried, and risen Saviour, the believer's death to sin, the burial of the old life, and the resurrection to walk in newness of life in Christ Jesus. It is a testimony to their faith in the final resurrection of the dead.

If you have never been baptized as a believer and you are a follower of Jesus, we invite you to make this public profession of your faith. If interested, please email us at riverside@riversideconnect.com. Check out the videos below for testimonies of some of those who have been baptized at Riverside...
