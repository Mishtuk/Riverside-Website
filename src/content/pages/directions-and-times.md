239 Columbia Avenue, Horsham, PA 19044

## Join us on Sunday Mornings

Sunday Services are held at 9:00 & 10:30am

Children's Classes and Livestream will be available during the 10:30am service.

Our Worship Service is available to watch online every Sunday. See [Livestream](/livestream).

## Parking

Visitors are invited to park in the parking lot behind the church building or in the small lot on Columbia Avenue directly across from the church. Additional parking is available along Homestead Lane behind the church building and in the field parking lot across the street from the church. The field parking lot can be accessed from Girard Avenue.
