*Each one must give as he has decided in his heart, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver. (2 Corinthians 9:7)*

Jesus was generous with us beyond measure. We should desire to be known as a generous church. Generosity is a language that cuts across all barriers and shows people that we are living out what we claim to believe.

## Online Donation

Use our online giving platform to make a one time gift or a recurring gift.

Our online giving platform will allow you to use different payment methods including credit card, debit card and even a check online. It's simple and secure, and only takes a few moments to go through the one-time setup process.

## Text Giving

Text an amount to **267-274-2425**.

First time users will be prompted to set up a giving method. To text to a specific fund, simply text an amount and the fund name (ex: $50 General)

## Ways to Give Without Fees

Offering Basket, Check by U.S. Mail, Online Bill Pay through Your Bank

Sending one-time or recurring payments through your bank's online bill-payment system is a convenient way to donate without fees. You can also use the offering basket during a worship service, or mail a check.

Please make checks payable to: **Riverside Community Church**
