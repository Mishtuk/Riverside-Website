Our Women's Ministry exists to support the leadership and overall ministry of Riverside Community Church. Women's Ministry complements our other Community Groups, offering a place for women to gather and study Scripture together. Our desire is to encourage and equip women to live fully in the plans and purposes God has for each woman. Discipling is a significant priority at Riverside, and our Women's Ministry aims to help women live out the gospel of Jesus Christ in their home, church and community, while embracing the perfect and unique design of womanhood. Life often becomes overloaded and busy as we attempt to balance our home life, marriage, career, and children. How can we hear God's quiet voice whispering to us amongst the clamor? Women's Ministry at Riverside is designed to delve into Scripture with other women, as we explore what God's Word says for our daily lives. All are welcome: wives, mothers, daughters, students, and women working in the home or outside the home.
